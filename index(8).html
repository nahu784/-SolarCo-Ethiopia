About RNTL

We are a professional logistics company

R N Trading and Logistics PLC (RNTL) is a trusted Ethiopian customs clearance, freight forwarding and road transit specialist, connecting the Djibouti, Berbera and Port Sudan corridors to Addis Ababa.

With a focus on operational efficiency, we specialize in delivering high-performing logistics services, including freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, and distribution.

Timely Delivery

Ensuring every shipment arrives safely and on schedule, providing reliable support that keeps your business operations efficient and uninterrupted.

Global Reach

Leveraging a vast network of global agents to secure the best shipping rates and seamless logistics solutions.

Safety & Quality

RNTL ensures safe, reliable, and high-quality service through strict safety standards and professional handling.

Supply Chain

A strong supply chain drives business success by ensuring efficient flow of goods and maximizing customer satisfaction.

Customs

Customs play a key role in international trade by ensuring smooth goods movement while enforcing regulations and protecting national interests.

Warehousing

RNTL delivers secure, modern warehousing with efficient handling and distribution, designed to meet each client's specific operational needs.
